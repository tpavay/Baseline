Navigation · how you reach this view

Proposal — a Log / Heart Rate toggle on the active workout

  1. ① While a workout is active and a strap is streaming, a segmented control appears at the top of the workout: LOG ↔ ♥ LIVE. The live option carries the current BPM + zone color, so heart rate is glanceable even from the log.
  2. ② Tapping ♥ LIVE (or swiping) switches the same screen to the full-screen HUD below. Tapping LOG returns to logging. No navigation push — it's one workout surface with two faces.
  3. ③ No strap, no tab. If nothing is streaming, the toggle is hidden and the workout looks exactly as it does today — this is the "no behavior change until go-live" guarantee.
  4. Scope note: issue #12 builds LiveHeartRateView standalone (the HUD + previews). Dropping it into the real workout surface with this toggle is the documented go-live step (live-heart-rate-go-live.md), since the workout views are owner-WIP. This mock is the intended target so the standalone view is shaped to fit it.
